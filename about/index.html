About LEGLOCK

What LEGLOCK is, and how it works

What LEGLOCK does

LEGLOCK is a prop research tool for the NBA, NFL, NHL and MLB. It tracks the top players in each league and shows how often their player props have gone over or under a line across their last 5, 10 and 20 games, calculated from real game logs. It also covers team props for every team in the four leagues, live scores and in-game stats, ESPN injury statuses, and full game-by-game logs for every tracked player.

The idea is simple: most prop research is arithmetic anyone could do, spread across too many pages to actually do it. Count how many of a player’s last ten games cleared a number, check whether their minutes are trending up or down, see who they play next — none of it is secret, all of it is tedious. LEGLOCK does the counting once, properly, and keeps it current.

Why it was built

LEGLOCK started as a personal spreadsheet: one player, one stat, a column of game logs and a hand-counted hit rate. The spreadsheet kept growing — more players, more stats, team totals, live games — until it clearly wanted to be a website. It is still built around the same question the spreadsheet answered: against this line, how has this player actually performed lately? Everything on the site exists to answer that question faster and more honestly.

How the numbers are made

  • Data. Game logs, box scores, schedules and injury statuses come from ESPN’s public feeds. The board refreshes from fresh logs whenever the site is opened or refreshed, and live data updates about every minute during games.
  • Research lines. Each prop’s line is set at the nearest half point to the player’s last-10 average for that stat. Lines can be stepped up or down in half-point steps, and every step recomputes the hit rates from the underlying game log.
  • Hit rates. For each window — last 5, last 10 and last 20 games — LEGLOCK counts the games in which the player finished over (or under) the line and expresses it as a percentage. Pushes and games not played are handled consistently, and the full game log behind every rate is one tap away on the Logs page, so any number on the site can be checked by hand.
  • Team props. Team totals, totals allowed and game totals use the same method, from the same logs, over the same windows.

Our honesty rules

Betting content has a trust problem, so LEGLOCK runs on three rules. First, research lines are always labelled as research lines — they are not sportsbook odds and we never present them as such. Second, every rate on the board can be verified against the raw game log, in the open, on this site. Third, we never describe any prop as a lock, a guarantee or free money. A 90% hit rate means a player cleared a number in nine of ten recent games. It also means they missed it once in ten — and the next game is a new game.

What LEGLOCK isn’t

LEGLOCK does not accept bets and is not a sportsbook. It is not affiliated with any sportsbook, league, team or ESPN, and it does not sell picks. Nothing on this site is betting or financial advice. It is a research and entertainment tool for adult sports fans, and it should be used alongside your own judgement — about matchups, minutes, injuries and everything else a hit rate cannot see.
